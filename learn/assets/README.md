# Assets & Treasury Savings Lessons

> Learn how to maintain the company's fixed asset register, calculate equipment depreciation, track digital and crypto holdings, and manage treasury reserves in PMG Hub.

---

## Overview

The Assets and Savings modules in `apps/admin` provide complete visibility into the company's capital strength:

1. **Asset Register (`/assets`)**: Physical office and tech hardware, domain portfolios, brand trademarks, depreciation schedules, and Luno cryptocurrency portfolio integration.
2. **Treasury Savings & Reserves (`/savings`)**: Business emergency funds, tax reserves, high-yield notice accounts, and interest accruals.
