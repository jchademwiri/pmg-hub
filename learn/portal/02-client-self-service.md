# 2. Client Self-Service & Billing

> Learn how clients navigate their dashboard, approve quotes, review invoices, download statements, and manage their business profile.

---

## 1. The Portal Dashboard

Upon signing in, the client is greeted by their personalized dashboard displaying:

- **Outstanding Balance & Due Date Banner**: Highlights open amounts and when they are due.
- **Active Projects & Tenders**: Shows ongoing work, progress percentages, and approaching milestones.
- **Recent Invoices & Quotes**: Quick access to recent billing documents.
- **Compliance Status Badge**: Visual indicator of whether their company compliance certificates are current or expiring soon.

---

## 2. Managing Quotes

- Clients navigate to `/quotes` to review pending project estimates.
- They can inspect individual line items, deliverables, and terms.
- **1-Click Acceptance**: Clicking **Accept Quote** updates the quote status in the Admin app and notifies the account manager to initiate project kickoff and billing.

---

## 3. Invoices & Payment Verification

- Under `/invoices`, clients see their full invoice history with clear status indicators (`Issued`, `Partially Paid`, `Paid`, `Overdue`).
- Clients can click **Download PDF** to obtain high-resolution tax invoice documents for their accounting departments.
- Bank account EFT details (specific to the billing division) are clearly rendered alongside payment references.

---

## 4. Live Statements of Account

- Under `/statements`, clients can generate a statement for any custom date range or download their standard monthly statement.
- The statement displays opening balances, invoices issued, EFT payments matched, credit notes, and closing balances with 30/60/90-day ageing buckets.

---

## 5. Projects & Tender Deliverables

- Under `/projects`, clients track deliverables across software development, digital marketing campaigns, or tender submissions.
- For tender engagements, clients can monitor submission closing dates, effort days completed, and review draft submission attachments.

---

## 6. Profile & Billing Settings

- Under `/profile`, clients can update:
  - Official business trading name and CIPC registration number.
  - VAT registration number.
  - Primary contact details and billing notification email address.
  - Physical and postal addresses for tax invoicing.
