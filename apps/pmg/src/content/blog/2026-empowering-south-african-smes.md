South African small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) contribute over 34% of the national GDP and provide employment to more than half of the formal workforce. However, SMEs face substantial operational hurdles: complex public procurement compliance regulations and the prohibitive cost of enterprise-grade digital engineering.

**Playhouse Media Group (PMG)** was founded to systematically dismantle these barriers through dedicated, specialized operating divisions.

---

## 1. TenderEdge Solutions: Demystifying Public Procurement

Public sector procurement represents hundreds of billions of rands annually. Through **TenderEdge Solutions**, we provide South African businesses with:

- End-to-end Central Supplier Database (CSD) management.
- CIDB contractor grading preparation and advancement.
- Turnkey Standard Bidding Document (SBD) compilation.
- Rigorous pre-flight administrative bid audits that prevent disqualification at gate zero.

---

## 2. Apex Web Solutions: Democratizing High-Performance Web Engineering

A business cannot compete nationally or globally with a slow, template-ridden website. Through **Apex Web Solutions**, we deliver:

- Ultra-fast, edge-deployed web applications built with Astro and Next.js.
- Clean, accessible user experience architecture (WCAG AA compliant).
- Search engine optimization that dominates both traditional search and AI discovery engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini).
- Transparent subscription-based pricing starting from R299/month, eliminating massive capital expenditure for early-stage companies.
