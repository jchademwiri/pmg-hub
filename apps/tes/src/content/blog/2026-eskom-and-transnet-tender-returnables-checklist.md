State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) such as **Eskom Holdings SOC Ltd** and **Transnet SOC Ltd** represent the largest single procurement spend in Southern Africa. However, over 65% of all bids submitted to these entities are disqualified during **Phase 1: Mandatory Returnables and Gatekeeping** before technical evaluators even review the methodology or pricing schedule.

In this guide, we break down the exact returnable documentation frameworks required by Eskom Tender Bulletins and Transnet e-Tenders, along with a master checklist to safeguard your submission against fatal compliance errors.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of SOE Gatekeeping Evaluations

Eskom and Transnet tender evaluations strictly follow a 4-phase sequential gatekeeping process:

```
[Phase 1: Mandatory Gatekeepers] ➔ [Phase 2: Functionality / Technical] ➔ [Phase 3: SHEQ & Financial] ➔ [Phase 4: Price & Specific Goals]
```

If a single mandatory returnable document in Phase 1 is omitted, expired, unsigned, or non-responsive, the tender system or bid committee is legally forbidden from considering the proposal further. There is no right of appeal or post-submission rectification for mandatory gate criteria.

## 2. Category A: Mandatory Commercial Returnables

The commercial file forms the foundational legal identity of your enterprise.

### Central Supplier Database (CSD) Master Report

- **CSD Registration Number**: Valid MAAA number required.
- **Tax Compliance Status (TCS)**: Must reflect "Compliant" on the day of bid closing.
- **Director Details**: All active CIPC directors must have verified ID numbers and tax numbers.
- **Government Employee Restriction**: Zero directors or shareholders may be employed by an organ of state (PERSAL checks are automatic).

### Standard Bidding Documents (SBD) / Tender Declarations

- **SBD 1**: Invitation to Bid with complete legal entity name matching CIPC records.
- **SBD 4**: Bidder's Disclosure Form detailing any conflict of interest or past restricted supplier listings.
- **SBD 6.1**: Preference Points Claim Form matching submitted B-BBEE affidavits or SANAS certificates.
- **Integrity Pact / Code of Ethics**: Transnet and Eskom each enforce their own proprietary Anti-Corruption and Integrity Declarations that must be signed and witnessed.

## 3. Category B: Safety, Health, Environment & Quality (SHEQ)

Both Eskom and Transnet enforce world-class occupational safety standards. Submitting a boilerplate health and safety plan will result in an immediate zero score.

### SHEQ Returnable Matrix

| Document Name                           | Eskom Requirement        | Transnet Requirement      | Common Rejection Reason                                                         |
| :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------- | :------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **COIDA / FEM Letter of Good Standing** | Mandatory valid letter   | Mandatory valid letter    | Submitting an expired COIDA certificate or proof of application without receipt |
| **Baseline Risk Assessment (BRA)**      | Site-specific matrix     | Scope-specific assessment | Generic generic copy-pasted hazard registers with wrong site names              |
| **SHE Policy & Objectives**             | Signed by MD/CEO         | Signed by MD/CEO          | Unsigned or outdated corporate safety policy statements                         |
| **Safety Officer Certification**        | SACPCMP registration     | SACPCMP or equivalent     | Appointing personnel without current professional council accreditation         |
| **ISO Certifications**                  | ISO 9001 / 45001 / 14001 | ISO 9001 quality manual   | Submitting expired accreditation or uncertified internal drafts                 |

## 4. Category C: Technical Track Record & Key Personnel

Phase 2 requires incontrovertible proof that your team has successfully delivered contracts of similar magnitude within the past 3 to 5 years.

### Client Reference Letters

- Must be on the official letterhead of the client organization.
- Must state the project description, contract value, start date, and completion date.
- Must include a clear assessment rating (e.g., "Satisfactory" or "Good").
- Must display the client contact person's name, designation, telephone number, and official email address. Purchase orders alone do **not** substitute for signed completion certificates.

### Curricula Vitae and Qualifications

- Detailed CVs for Contracts Manager, Site Engineers, Safety Officers, and Quality Inspectors.
- Certified copies of professional qualifications (ECSA, SACPCMP, PMI, etc.).
- Certifications must typically be certified by a Commissioner of Oaths within the last 3 months.

## 5. Master Submission Pre-Flight Checklist

Before sealing physical tender boxes at Eskom Megawatt Park or submitting through the Transnet e-Tender submission portal:

- [ ] **TCS Pin**: Verified as green and valid on SARS eFiling 24 hours prior to deadline.
- [ ] **Pricing Schedule**: Separate envelope or uploaded file (if two-envelope bidding system is invoked).
- [ ] **All Addenda Acknowledged**: Check Eskom Tender Bulletin 24 hours before closing for last-minute clarifications or closing date extensions.
- [ ] **Signatures & Witnesses**: Verify that every page requiring initialing has been signed by an authorized director (per resolution).
- [ ] **Document Format**: PDF documents flattened, text-searchable, and unencrypted with file sizes under portal upload thresholds (typically 30MB per batch).
